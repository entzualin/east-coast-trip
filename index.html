Hualien–Taitung Road Trip

A family loop down the west side, across the southern mountains to Taitung, up the rice-field valley and the Pacific coast road, then home through Yilan. We have a hired driver, and our 4-year-old gets to bike most days.

Bade 八德→South Link→ Taitung 臺東→rift valley→ Taitung→coast road→ Hualien 花蓮→Su-Hua Hwy→Bade
Sun 18 & Mon 192 nights in Taitung city (same hotel)
Tue 20 & Wed 212 nights in Hualien city (same hotel)

  • Day 2 · Mon 19Ranch & rice fields
  • Day 3 · Tue 20The coast road
  • Day 4 · Wed 21Ocean park

All times are rough. Drive times assume normal traffic. Stops marked Optional are fillers: do them if everyone has energy, and skip them if not. Road numbers: 國3 = national freeway, 台9 = provincial highway.

Day1

Sunday 18 October

Down the west side and over the South Link to Taitung

About 430 km · roughly 6 hours of driving plus stops · Night: Taitung

國3→台17台1→台9 南迴

  1. 07:00
    Leave Bade

    Join Freeway 3 southbound at Daxi interchange 大溪交流道. Southbound traffic is light early on a Sunday.

  2. ~1 hr 45 · 國3
  3. 08:45
    Qingshui rest stop 清水服務區

    Breakfast, toilets, a stretch. It sits on a hill looking out over Taichung harbour.

  4. ~1 hr 30 · 國3
  5. 10:30
    Dongshan rest stop 東山服務區

    Second wiggle break before the last stretch south.

  6. ~1 hr 30 · 國3 to the end at Linbian, then 台17/台1
  7. 12:00
    Lunch in Fangliao 枋寮

    Seafood near the fishing harbour, or a simple lunch box. This is the last big town before the mountain crossing.

  8. ~1 hr 15 · 台1 → 台9 南迴
  9. 14:15
    Duoliang Station 多良車站

    An old station on a cliff right above the Pacific, often called Taiwan's prettiest. There's a short, steep walk up from the parking. 20–30 min.

  10. ~50 min · 台9
  11. 15:30
    Check in, Taitung city

    A short rest after the long drive.

  12. 16:15
    Taitung Sugar Factory 臺東糖廠

    Old sugar-mill buildings with small craft shops and space to wander. Easy for little legs. About 1 hour.

  13. 18:00
    Taitung Tourist Night Market 臺東觀光夜市

    Only runs on Sunday nights, on Zhengqi Road 正氣路. Afterwards, if anyone still has energy, it's a short walk to Tiehua Village 鐵花村 for lanterns and live music.

If plans change

  • Freeway 3 slow: switch to Freeway 1 國1 using a cross-link such as 國10 or 國8.
  • Arriving late: move the sugar factory to Day 2 morning. It's on the way out of town.
  • Rain: swap the sugar factory for the National Museum of Prehistory 史前館. It's indoors, good with kids, and open on Sundays until about 17:00.
  • South Link 台9 closed (rare, usually typhoons): there is no good detour. Run the trip in reverse instead (see "Typhoon or South Link closure" below).
Day2

Monday 19 October

East Rift Valley: ranch, grassy plateau and golden rice fields

About 130 km round trip · roughly 2.5 hours of driving · Night: Taitung (same hotel)

台9north, then south again

  1. 08:30
    Leave hotel
  2. ~30 min · 台9
  3. 09:00
    Chulu Ranch 初鹿牧場

    Feed the calves, run on the hillside lawns, and have fresh milk and ice cream. Go early, before the day warms up. About 2 hours.

  4. ~15 min
  5. 11:15
    Luye Highland 鹿野高地

    A wide grassy plateau with valley views. The balloon festival is over by October, but paragliders are sometimes flying. 30–45 min.

  6. 12:00
    Lunch in Luye / Longtian 鹿野・龍田
  7. 13:00
    Longtian village bike loop 龍田村 Optional

    Flat, quiet lanes through an old village and tea fields, with rentals nearby. 45–60 min. Skip it to save energy for Chishang.

  8. ~40 min · 台9 · good nap window
  9. 15:00
    Bike Mr. Brown Avenue, Chishang 池上 伯朗大道

    Straight lanes through rice fields that should be golden in mid-October, with mountains on both sides. Shops at the entrance rent kids' bikes, child seats and 4-wheel family carts. Pass the famous lone tree 金城武樹. 1.5–2 hours; the light is best late afternoon.

  10. 17:15
    Dinner: a Chishang rice lunch box 池上便當

    Chishang is famous for its rice. Eat in town before heading back.

  11. ~1 hr · 台9 south
  12. 19:00
    Back at the Taitung hotel

    Same hotel, so no packing tonight.

If plans change

  • Tired kid: drop Longtian and get to Chishang earlier for a longer, slower ride.
  • Rain: the ranch is fine in light rain. Swap the Chishang ride for the indoor Chishang Lunchbox Museum 池上飯包文化故事館 or the Chishang Granary Art Museum 池上穀倉藝術館.
  • Remember the National Museum of Prehistory is closed on Mondays.
Day3

Tuesday 20 October

The coast road from Taitung to Hualien

About 180 km · roughly 4 hours of driving plus stops · Night: Hualien

台11north the whole way

  1. 08:00
    Check out, Taitung
  2. ~10 min · 台11
  3. 08:15
    Xiaoyeliu 小野柳

    Odd tofu-block and honeycomb rocks along the shore, with an easy boardwalk. 30 min.

  4. ~10 min
  5. 09:00
    Jialulan 加路蘭

    Driftwood art on a seaside lawn. A quick photo stop and run-around. 20 min.

  6. ~10 min
  7. 09:30
    Duli visitor center lawn 都歷遊客中心 Optional

    A big lawn with a sea view and good toilets. A handy break before the longer stretch.

  8. ~45 min · 台11
  9. 10:30
    Sanxiantai 三仙台

    Walk the eight-arch footbridge out to the islet. The bridge has a lot of steps, so expect to carry your little one for part of it. 1–1.5 hours. Bring sun hats.

  10. 12:00
    Lunch in Chenggong 成功

    Seafood near the fishing harbour. Swordfish 旗魚 is the local catch.

  11. ~15 min
  12. 13:15
    Stone Umbrella 石雨傘

    An umbrella-shaped rock at the end of a short coastal walk. 30 min.

  13. ~30 min
  14. 14:15
    Jingang Avenue, Changbin 長濱 金剛大道 Optional

    A short detour inland to a dead-straight road through terraced rice fields that runs down to the sea. 30 min.

  15. ~30 min · 台11
  16. 15:15
    Tropic of Cancer marker 北回歸線標誌（靜浦）

    A 10-minute photo stop.

  17. 15:30
    Changhong Bridge 長虹橋

    A red arch bridge at the mouth of the Xiuguluan River, looking out to a small island. 20–30 min.

  18. ~45 min · 台11
  19. 16:30
    Baqi Lookout 芭崎眺望台

    A clifftop view over a sweeping bay, and the last coastal stop. 15 min.

  20. ~45 min
  21. 17:30
    Check in, Hualien city

    Dinner at Dongdamen Night Market 東大門夜市.

If plans change

  • Part of 台11 closed (landslides or high waves after a typhoon): cut inland on the nearest crossover and carry on north on 台9 to Hualien. See the crossover table below. Prefer 台30, and avoid County Road 64.
  • Running late: skip Duli and Jingang Avenue first.
  • Rain or strong wind: the coast still looks great from the car. Shorten the stops and skip the Sanxiantai bridge in heavy wind.
Day4

Wednesday 21 October

Farglory Ocean Park

About 30 min each way from Hualien city · Night: Hualien (same hotel)

台11south from the city, and back

  1. 09:30
    Leave hotel
  2. ~30 min · 台11
  3. 10:00
    Farglory Ocean Park 遠雄海洋公園

    Check the show times (dolphins, sea lions) at the gate and plan rides around them. It's much quieter on a weekday. Check opening hours the day before.

  4. 16:30
    Leave the park, rest at the hotel
  5. 17:15
    Qixingtan beach 七星潭 Optional

    A crescent pebble beach 15 min from the city. Throwing stones into the sea is great fun for a 4-year-old.

  6. 18:30
    Dinner in Hualien city

    Dongdamen Night Market if you skipped it on Day 3.

If plans change

  • Heavy rain: the park's indoor aquarium areas stay open, but outdoor rides and shows may stop. Indoor alternatives in town include the Hualien Cultural & Creative Park 花蓮文化創意產業園區 and the Qixing Bonito Museum 七星柴魚博物館.
  • Want a slower day: do half a day at the park and spend the afternoon at the beach.
Day5

Thursday 22 October

Home via the Su-Hua Highway and Yilan

About 230 km · roughly 4 hours of driving plus stops · Home

台9 蘇花改→國5→國3

  1. 08:30
    Check out, Hualien
  2. ~25 min south
  3. 09:00
    Liyu Lake 鯉魚潭 Optional

    Swan pedal boats, then a ride on the flat lakeside path. 1.5 hours. Skip it to get home about 1.5 hours earlier.

  4. ~1 hr · 台9 蘇花改
  5. 11:30
    Qingshui Cliffs viewpoint 清水斷崖 If open

    Sheer cliffs dropping straight into the Pacific. 15–20 min. Ask the driver whether it's open, because the area was affected by the 2024 earthquake.

  6. ~1 hr · 台9 蘇花改
  7. 12:45
    Lunch at Nanfang'ao harbour, Su'ao 蘇澳 南方澳

    Fresh seafood by the fishing port.

  8. ~25 min
  9. 14:00
    Meihua Lake 梅花湖

    A flat bike loop around a small lake, with rentals at the lake. A good last ride. 1–1.5 hours.

  10. ~1 hr 45 · 國5 via the Hsuehshan Tunnel → 國3 → Daxi interchange
  11. 15:30
    Leave Yilan

    Aim to be on Freeway 5 by about 15:30. Thursday afternoon northbound is usually smooth.

  12. 17:15
    Home in Bade

If plans change

  • Hsuehshan Tunnel jammed: take the coastal road 台2 via Toucheng → Fulong → Keelung (scenic, but longer). Avoid 台9 北宜公路: it's very twisty and bad for carsick kids.
  • Su-Hua Highway closed (rockfall or typhoon): the family takes the train from Hualien to Taipei (about 2–2.5 hrs) and the driver brings the car back once the road reopens. Driving back the long way through Taitung takes 7+ hours.
  • Rain: swap Meihua Lake for the Lanyang Museum 蘭陽博物館 in Toucheng, which is on the way to the freeway, or the National Center for Traditional Arts 傳藝中心 in Wujie.

Typhoon or South Link closure

If a typhoon is forecast for the start of the trip or the South Link is closed on Day 1, run the loop in reverse:

  1. Bade → 國3/國5 → 台9 蘇花改 → Hualien (about 3.5 hrs). Liyu Lake in the afternoon.
  2. Farglory Ocean Park.
  3. Coast road 台11 south to Taitung (Day 3 in reverse).
  4. Rift valley day: Chulu Ranch, Luye, Chishang.
  5. Taitung → 台9 南迴 → 國3 → Bade (about 6 hrs).

If the typhoon is a big one, it may be better to move the dates.

If we need to cut things on the day

Drop these first, in this order: Longtian bike loop → Duli lawn → Jingang Avenue → Liyu Lake → Qingshui Cliffs.
